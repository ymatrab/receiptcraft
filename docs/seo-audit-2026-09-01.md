# Full SEO Audit — makecepeit.com
**Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Health Score: 83/100** · Crawl: 536 of 1,130 URLs · Data: GSC (Aug 4–31), Bing WMT (66d)

## Executive summary

The site is technically close to flawless and commercially close to invisible. Those
are not in tension — they are the same finding seen twice.

Across 536 crawled pages there are **zero** missing titles, meta descriptions,
canonicals, H1s, og:images or schema blocks; zero duplicate titles or descriptions;
and zero of 1,887 images missing alt text. There is nothing left to fix in the
tags.

Meanwhile: 41,189 Google impressions in 28 days produced **292 clicks (0.71%)**, and
the 434 queries that describe what the product actually *is* — "receipt maker",
"receipt generator", "receipt template", "make a receipt" — sit at **average
position 56.3** and produced **3 clicks**.

The content estate ranks for an adjacent topic, not for the product.

## Critical findings

### 1. Page-1 rankings that cannot convert (≈9,800 impressions, 11 clicks)

23 pages rank in the top 10 with ≥150 impressions and CTR below 0.5%. Their queries
are **retrieval intent** — people trying to get their *own* receipt back from a
merchant:

| Page | Impr | Pos | Clicks | Dominant query |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| /receipt-help/zara-return-policy | 1,655 | 8.0 | 0 | "zara recover my receipt" (pos 6.0) |
| /receipt-help/sephora-lost-receipt | 1,077 | 8.6 | 0 | "sephora receipt lookup" (pos 7.0) |
| /receipt-help/zara-receipt-copy | 851 | 8.1 | 0 | — |
| /blog/amount-tendered-meaning | 842 | 5.5 | 2 | "amount tendered meaning" (pos 3.7) |
| /blog/how-to-write-tip-on-receipt | 723 | 10.1 | 0 | — |
| /blog/do-receipts-show-card-number | 434 | 9.9 | 0 | — |

**Confirmed on a second engine.** Bing shows the identical shape on queries where
AI Overviews are rare: "walmart receipt lookup" — 464 impressions, **position 8,
0 clicks**; "sam's club receipt look up" — position 4, 0 clicks; "zara receipt
retrival" — position 4, 0 clicks.

Two engines, same behaviour, rules out "an AI Overview ate the click" as the whole
story. The mechanism is simpler: someone searching *"zara recover my receipt"* wants
zara.com. A third-party receipt **generator** is the wrong answer, so they don't
click — and on the rare click they cannot convert, because the site cannot produce
their real Zara receipt. This traffic is structurally un-monetizable.

`amount tendered meaning` at **position 3.7 with 0 of 118 clicks** is the
definitional variant of the same problem: the SERP answers it outright.

`/create` is **not the problem**: it has a correct title ("Free Receipt Maker —
Create a Receipt Online"), a matching H1, 875 words of server-rendered copy and
FAQ schema. It is well-built and ranks 77th.

**On-page is maxed out; the binding constraint is off-page authority.** Bing
Webmaster Tools reports **no inbound links** for the domain (`TotalPages: 0`). That
is one source and should be confirmed against a second, but it fits the evidence
exactly: the site ranks 5–10 for anything uncontested and 70–88 for anything
contested. No amount of further on-page work moves a head term without links.

### 3. "receipt scanner app" is a different product

`/blog/best-receipt-scanning-apps` (610 impr, pos 76.6) and the ~250 impressions on
scanner queries target people who want to **scan** existing receipts. The site
**generates** receipts. Even ranked, that traffic does not convert.

## What is genuinely excellent

- **Technical (95/100).** Sitemap correctly split into 7 section indexes so GSC
  reports coverage per section; per-page `lastModified` constants with written
  justification for every shared bump; HSTS preload, CSP, `nosniff`,
  Permissions-Policy; p50 response 0.51s, p50 HTML 75KB.
- **Schema (96/100).** Organization + WebSite on all 536 pages, BreadcrumbList on
  522, FAQPage on 410, HowTo on 111. **Zero parse errors.**
- **AI search (88/100).** `llms.txt` + 71KB `llms-full.txt`; all of OAI-SearchBot,
  ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, CCBot explicitly allowed. The
  llms.txt is honest about limits and cites issuing authorities.
- **Images (98/100).** 1,887 images, **0** missing alt text.
- **Trust.** `/create` says "No sign-up to start; sign in to download" — accurate,
  and a fix on the false no-signup copy flagged in the July audit.

## Limits of this audit

- **No CrUX/field CWV** — no PageSpeed API key present. Performance scored from
  crawl transfer timings only, not real-user LCP/INP/CLS.
- **Crawl is a stratified sample** — 536 of 1,130 URLs (all of core, compare,
  templates; sampled brands, examples, receipt-help, blog).
- **Backlinks from one source** (Bing WMT). DataForSEO is on hold at the owner's
  request and was not called; Semrush API balance is exhausted.
- 47 pages initially timed out under concurrency and were refetched successfully —
  all 536 returned HTTP 200. No genuine errors, redirects or broken pages found.
