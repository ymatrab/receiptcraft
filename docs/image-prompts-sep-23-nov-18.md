## How to use

Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** first, then the **CONTENT BLOCK** for the image you
want, as one prompt. The style block is identical every time; it is what keeps
the set looking like one family.

Fictional organisations only: `Helping Hands Foundation`, `Grace Community
Church`, `Harbor & Main`, `Northgate Supply Co.`, `Ridgeline Auto Care`,
`Summit Towing & Recovery`, `Maple Street Fuel`, `Bayview Suites`, `Cedar Court
Apartments`, `Lakeside Cottage`, `Metro Park Garage`, `CityLine Cab Co.`,
`Little Oaks Daycare`, `Brightside Plumbing`, `Coolbreeze Heating & Air`,
`Parkview Dental`, `Riverside Family Clinic`, `Fix-It Handyman Services`,
`The Copper Table`. Never substitute a real business name.

**Brand posts** (Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, Target, AutoZone, Costco, Uber,
StockX, GOAT, Amazon, Apple Store, Best Buy, CVS): the brand name appears
**only as plain headline text** in the house style. The receipt itself shows
the grey placeholder `[STORE NAME]`, never the brand's logo, colours, fonts or
receipt design, and heroes carry the footer "Recreated for personal records."
If the model adds a logo anyway, regenerate.

**Inline images (#11 onward):** the posts are not written yet. Whoever writes
each post writes its inline alt text to describe what the `-2` image shows.

  typography throughout. No people, no real brand logos, no barcodes unless
