# Nano Banana image prompts — Sep 22–25 batch (16 images)

Two per post: `<slug>.jpeg` (hero banner) and `<slug>-2.jpeg` (inline comparison
or detail). Output 16:9, as large as the model allows. Save into `Posts/M9/`
using the filenames in each heading.

## How to use

Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** first, then the **CONTENT BLOCK** for the image you
want, as one prompt. The style block is identical every time — it is what keeps
the sixteen images looking like one set.

Fictional organisations only: `Helping Hands Foundation`, `Grace Community
Church`, `Harbor & Main`, `Northgate Supply Co.` Never substitute a real
charity, church or business name.

---

## STYLE BLOCK — paste before every content block

```
A wide 16:9 editorial banner illustration in a clean corporate infographic
style, soft studio lighting, very light blue-white gradient background with
faint pale geometric shapes.

Palette: deep navy #1B2A4E for headlines and body text, sky blue for secondary
text and icons, mint green #4ECDA4 for highlighted total rows and underline
accents, white cards, cream #FFFEFB receipt paper.

Layout rules that apply to every image in this set:
- Headline centred at the top on two lines: line 1 in heavy bold navy, line 2
  in lighter medium-weight slate grey.
- Beneath the headline, a letterspaced small-caps strapline in soft blue, with
  a short mint-green underline accent centred below it.
- Handwritten blue script accent phrases in the upper-left and upper-right
  corners, two or three short lines each.
- A row of three small navy line icons with short caps labels along the bottom
  left, separated by thin vertical dividers.
- Bottom-right, the logo: a small blue bar-chart mark, then the wordmark
  "makecepeit" with "make" in bold navy and "cepeit" in lighter blue, and
  beneath it in tiny letterspaced caps:
  "PRACTICAL FINANCE FOR A BRIGHTER YOU"
- Soft matte 3D props framing the composition, never overlapping the text.
- All rendered text must be spelled exactly as given. Crisp, legible
  typography throughout. No people, no real brand logos, no barcodes unless
  asked for.
```
