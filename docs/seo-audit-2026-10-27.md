sitemap URLs (every one 200), Bing Webmaster API, live Microlaunch pages.
Read-only — nothing changed on the site.

## 1. Internal links — what the crawl shows

| Section | Pages | Content inlinks (median) | Where its links go |
|---|---|---|---|
| /brands | 352 | 6 (max 15) | brands 1,760 · blog 1,408 · create 704 · templates 360 |
| /templates | 56 | 7 | templates 280 · blog 220 · brands 139 |
| /receipt-help | 219 | 4 | blog 876 · create 657 · brands 438 · **templates 0** |
| /blog | 230 | 12 (44 posts ≤ 2) | blog 792 · create 475 · templates 148 · **brands 22** |
| /create | 1 | **1,161** | — |

Five findings, in order of value:

1. **Blog posts don't link up to the page they support.** The hub map in
   `lib/related-posts.ts` only links hub → post. A post's own links go to
   `/create` ("Open the receipt maker"). 230 posts link brand pages 22 times
   in total. `/blog/walmart-receipt-generator` and
   `/blog/printable-walmart-receipt-generator` rank p29–37 for "walmart receipt
   generator" next to `/brands/walmart` at p7, splitting the query instead of
   feeding it.
2. **The hub map is stale.** It sends the pharmacy, veterinary, florist, gym,
   dry-cleaning, towing, electronics, clothing and hardware posts to `/create`.
   Each of those now has its own template page. "pharmacy receipt maker" is
   split between `/templates/pharmacy` p19, `/templates/medical-receipt` p36
   and the pharmacy post p34.
3. **Receipt-help never links a template.** 219 pages, 51% of impressions and
   most AI citations, link only to their brand page, sibling guides, posts and
   `/create?template=…`. That last one canonicalises to `/create`, which
   doesn't need more links (1,161 already, still p76 for "receipt maker").
4. **The best earners are under-linked.** `/brands/paypal` makes 134 of the
   site's clicks at p8.9 on 6 inlinks, all from other brand pages.
   `/brands/starbucks` (p18–20, 340 impressions/90d) and `/brands/uber` (p35–39)
   have 12.
5. **Brand queries go to the wrong page.** "chipotle receipt generator" ranks
   the `/brands` hub at p25, not `/brands/chipotle`. "mcdonalds receipt
   generator" ranks a receipt-help guide at p32. "kroger receipt template"
   ranks a receipt-help guide and an example page.

**What internal links won't do:** move "receipt maker"/"receipt generator".
`/create` already gets 1,161 internal links with "receipt maker" as the main
anchor (410×). The `/blog/best-free-receipt-generator` post outranks it
(p68 vs p76–86). That ceiling is authority, per the 2026-10-19 verdict.
Internal links lift mid-tail brand and template pages at p8–40, and that's
where the clicks are (brand pages: 5.1% CTR).

### Fixes (code, one branch)

| # | Change | File |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Each blog post gets one contextual "up" link to its hub page, with an exact-match anchor ("Walmart receipt generator"). The hub is derived from: reverse hub map → brand slug in post slug → template keyword. | `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx`, `lib/related-posts.ts` |
| L2 | Move the stale `/create` spokes to their template hubs | `lib/related-posts.ts` |
| L3 | Each receipt-help page links its category template (Whole Foods → grocery store receipt template) | `app/receipt-help/[slug]/page.tsx`, `lib/topic-clusters.ts` |
| L4 | Point the receipt-help main CTA at `/brands/{slug}` instead of `/create?template=` | `app/receipt-help/[slug]/page.tsx` |
| L5 | Give the top earners (paypal, walmart, amazon, starbucks, uber, instacart, home-depot) links from the relevant template hub and topical posts | `lib/topic-clusters.ts` |
| L6 | Link the 24 examples with zero content inlinks from their brand page | examples routes |

Check: pull GSC after 3–4 weeks for the brand and template pages L1–L5 touch.
Look at position and clicks against this 90-day baseline. Leading indicator:
"walmart/starbucks/pharmacy" queries consolidate onto one URL.
**It failed if:** the posts and owner pages still split the same queries
after 4 weeks.

## 2. GEO / AEO

All AI crawlers get a 200 with identical HTML. The problem is what they read.

- **High — llms-full.txt lists 0 of 352 brand pages and no /alternatives.**
  It lists ~230 posts, many retrieval-intent. So AI learns we help people
  *find* receipts. Fix: `app/llms-full.txt/route.ts`, `app/llms.txt/route.ts`.
- **High — receipt-help FAQs answer return-policy questions only.** Add "Can I
  make a replacement {Brand} receipt?" with an answer that names Makecepeit and
  links the brand page. AI Overviews already quote these passages (42 of 72
  citations). File: `lib/intent-pages.ts`.
- **High — brand pages have no quotable passage.** `/brands/walmart` runs
  469 words with no 40–60-word "what this is" block and no question H2s. One
  template covers all 352 pages: `app/brands/[slug]/page.tsx`.
- **Medium — /pricing has no prose answer** ("$0 to build; clean download is a
  one-time $3 7-day pass, $7.99/month or $49/year; nothing auto-renews").
  llms-full says "$3/week", which reads like a subscription.
- **Medium — one post still knocks competitors for watermarks and paywalls at
  download**, which we now do ("Best Receipt Generator Apps: 5 Free Tools
  Compared"). Lives in Sanity.
- **Owner — entity.** `sameAs` lists only x.com. Profiles on Product Hunt,
  AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, G2 or Capterra, Crunchbase and YouTube, plus real
  Reddit answers, are the only lever on the trust gap. Reddit is co-cited in 41
  of 72 AI Overviews.
